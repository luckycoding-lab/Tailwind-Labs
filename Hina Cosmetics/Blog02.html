Description

Neem, also known as Indian lilac, has been revered in traditional skincare for centuries due to its powerful antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. Our Neem Face Wash leverages these benefits to give you a clean, clear, and refreshed complexion.

Key Benefit

  • Deep Cleansing: Our Neem Face Wash effectively removes dirt, excess oil, and impurities from your skin, preventing clogged pores and reducing the risk of acne breakouts.
  • Anti-Acne Action: Neem's natural antibacterial properties help to combat acne-causing bacteria, reduce inflammation, and soothe irritated skin.
  • Natural Hydration: Unlike harsh chemical cleansers, our Neem Face Wash maintains your skin’s natural moisture balance, leaving it hydrated and soft.
  • Skin Purification: Neem's purifying properties help detoxify your skin, promoting a clearer and more radiant complexion.

How to Use Hina Cosmetics Face Pack.

Using our face pack is simple and easy:

  • 1)Cleanse your face thoroughly with a gentle cleanser.
  • 2) Mix the face pack with rose water or plain water to form a smooth paste.
  • 3) Apply an even layer to your face, avoiding the eye area.
  • 4) Leave it on for 15-20 minutes or until it dries completely.
  • 5) Rinse off with lukewarm water, gently massaging in circular motions.
  • 6) Pat your face dry and follow up with your favorite moisturizer.
